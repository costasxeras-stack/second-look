# 0002: Use titles as a proxy for visible fitment

**Status:** accepted, 0.1.0

## Context

I cannot check true fitment for each part without a catalogue.

## Decision

Count what the shopper can see on the results page: whether the title names the car, front or rear. Say clearly that a title without the car may still fit.

## Tradeoff

- Undercounts parts that fit
- Measures the shopper's real problem: they cannot tell from the page
